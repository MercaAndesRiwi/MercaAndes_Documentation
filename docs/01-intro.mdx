# MercaAndes Omnichannel

**MercaAndes** is a data platform for sales, inventory, and marketplace analytics. The project integrates point-of-sale transactions, marketplace events, and seller inventories into a single source of information for commercial, logistics, and customer-service areas.

## Business context

MercaAndes S.A.S. is a retail chain operating in five Andean countries: Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, and Chile. The operation includes 140 physical stores, its own marketplace, and third-party sellers who publish and sell products on the platform.

The digital channel has gained share of the business: over the last two years it grew from 12% to 38% of sales. This growth increased the volume and variety of information generated at the point-of-sale systems, the marketplace, and the external sellers' operations.

Each channel relies on different data sources and systems. Point-of-sale transactions are recorded in country-specific relational databases, the marketplace holds order information, and sellers report their inventories through an API. The platform integrates these sources to provide a consistent view of net sales, returns, and available inventory.

## Problem to solve

MercaAndes faces difficulties in obtaining timely visibility of sales behavior and real product availability:

- Around 6% of sales is lost to stockouts that customers detect before the company does.
- Different areas calculate different sales figures, so there is no single reference for total sales.
- Promotions are activated on products without stock, causing cancellations and negative reviews.
- Seller-reported inventories can be outdated, and seller performance lacks a uniform measurement.
- Some stores hold excess inventory while others show shortages.

Commercial reports are prepared manually, depend on a single person, and can take up to 48 hours. The core problem is not the absence of data but its dispersion across different systems and formats, and the lack of a tim construct that turns it into information useful for the business.

## Objectives

The MercaAndes data platform targets the following business objectives:

1. Reduce undetected stockouts by 40% during the first three months of operation.
2. Consolidate net sales, returns, and available inventory into a single source of information.
3. Generate imminent-stockout alerts with at least 24 hours of anticipation.
4. Measure and classify external seller performance using objective criteria.
5. Provide a daily commercial dashboard, updated without manual intervention.
6. Keep same-day information with a maximum delay of 15 minutes.
